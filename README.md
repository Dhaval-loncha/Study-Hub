# Study Hub

**Study Hub** is an open-source online platform designed specifically for GTU (Gujarat Technological University) degree engineering students in the Computer Science and IT branches. It provides easy access to study materials, notes, and resources relevant to their subjects, enabling students to enhance their learning experience.

---

## 🌟 Features:
- **Subject-Specific Resources**: Organized materials and notes tailored to various engineering subjects for Computer Science and IT students.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easy navigation and clean design for an enhanced user experience.
- **Responsive Design**: Fully responsive layout to ensure optimal access across different devices including mobile, tablet, and desktop.

---

## 💻 Technologies Used:
- **HTML5** and **CSS3** for structuring and styling the platform.
- **JavaScript** for dynamic interactions and enhancing user experience.

---

## 🎯 Purpose:
The primary goal of this project is to provide GTU students with a centralized platform for accessing study materials and resources for their engineering courses. This platform aims to support academic success by providing useful and relevant content.
